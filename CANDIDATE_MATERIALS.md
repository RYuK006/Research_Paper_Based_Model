### Candidate CAND_001: `(La0.85Y0.15)H10`
- **Discovery Gap Origin**: Untried element pair La-Y from literature co-occurrence analysis (Score: 350.0)
- **Physical Verification**: Status `HIGHLY_PROMISING` (Score: 0.95)
- **Verification Notes**: Favorable polar-covalent / metallic hybridization for electronic transport.; High precompression index (0.99): Dopant cations induce steric pressure, stabilizing H-clathrate cage at reduced external pressure.

#### Experimental Synthesis Recipe
- **Method**: **Laser-heated diamond anvil cell (LH-DAC)**
- **Starting Precursors**:
  - High-purity (La, Y) metal foil or powder (purity $\ge 99.99\%$), stoichiometrically weighed.
  - Ammonia borane (NH3BH3) or pure molecular H2 gas loading as clean hydrogen donor.
  - Rhenium or tungsten gasket pre-indented to 25-35 $\mu$m thickness with 100 $\mu$m sample chamber.
- **Synthesis Conditions**:
  - Pressure: 159.8 GPa (monitored via diamond anvil Raman / ruby fluorescence)
  - Temperature: 1800 - 2200 K (double-sided pulsed Nd:YAG laser heating at $\lambda=1064$ nm)
  - Duration: 10 - 20 minutes laser annealing to induce cage phase nucleation
- **Characterization Plan**:
  - In-situ Synchrotron Powder X-ray Diffraction ($\lambda=0.434$ Å) to confirm Fm-3m (#225) clathrate lattice formation.
  - Four-probe electrical resistance using sputtered Pt/Ti microelectrodes deposited on diamond culet.
  - Measure resistance transition: verify zero-resistance state in predicted Tc window (245.0 - 280.0 K).
  - Magnetic susceptibility / AC susceptibility to confirm Meissner diamagnetic shielding.
- **Safety & Hazards**:
  - ⚠️ Extreme pressure risk: Diamond anvil culet cleavage during laser pulsing; operate behind blast shielding.
  - ⚠️ Pressurized hydrogen embrittlement: Inspect gasket seal integrity prior to heating.

- **Synthesis Conditions**:
  - Pressure: 0.2 - 0.4 mbar dynamic O2 partial pressure during ablation; 1 atm post-anneal

- **Characterization Plan**:
  - High-resolution XRD $\theta-2\theta$ scans to verify c-axis epitaxial orientation and space group Pmmm (#47).

  - SQUID vibrating sample magnetometry (VSM) to quantify superconducting volume fraction.
- **Safety & Hazards**:
  - ⚠️ UV Excimer Laser radiation hazard: Class 4 laser safety goggles required during PLD ablation.

#### Experimental Synthesis Recipe
- **Method**: **High-Temperature Flux Growth & Arc Melting**
- **Starting Precursors**:
  - High-purity elemental ingots of As, Co, Fe, Fe.
  - Self-flux medium (e.g. excess FeAs or Sn flux) inside evacuated quartz/alumina ampoule.
- **Synthesis Conditions**:
  - Pressure: 2.0 GPa (or sealed evacuated tube)
  - Temperature: 1150 °C for 24h, followed by slow cooling at 2-4 °C/hour down to 750 °C
  - Duration: 7 to 10 days crystal growth cycle
- **Characterization Plan**:
  - Single-crystal X-ray diffraction (SC-XRD) to refine tetragonal structure (P4/nmm (#129)).

  - Temperature-dependent electrical resistivity and Hall effect to probe carrier density.
- **Safety & Hazards**:
  - ⚠️ Toxic volatile vapors (e.g. As / Se sublimation) if ampoule ruptures at 1150 °C: Handle strictly in negative-pressure fume hood.
  - ⚠️ Thermal stress ampoule explosion risk during furnace ramp-down.
